# SportSync
**Find Players. Play Together.**

## The Problem: The court is ready. The players aren't.
Recreational players and sports enthusiasts often struggle to find enough players to form complete teams. Coordinating across messy social media group chats leads to unanswered messages, last-minute cancellations, and wasted court reservation fees. Players are also hesitant to invite strangers due to skill mismatches and safety concerns.

## The Solution
SportSync is a local sports matchmaking web application that connects solo players or incomplete teams based on their skill level, sport preference, schedule availability, and location. 

Instead of traditional facility-first booking apps that assume you already have a full team, SportSync focuses on **complete roster matchmaking**—solving the human assembly problem first.

### Core MVP Features
* **Smart Matchmaking & Filtering:** Discover nearby games with available player slots, filtered by sport (e.g., Badminton, Basketball, Volleyball), time, and location.
* **Verified Trust Profiles:** Basic player profiles displaying sport preferences and skill tiers to build trust and ensure compatible matchups.
* **Roster Completion (Direct Booking):** Organizers can post open slots, and nearby players can instantly RSVP to complete the lineup before securing the venue.
